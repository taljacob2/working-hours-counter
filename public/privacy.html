Privacy Policy — Work Hours Logger

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Work Hours Logger ("the app") is a personal work-hours tracking tool. This page explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

What we collect

  • Account info — your email address, used to identify your account. If you sign in with Google or GitHub, we receive the email address and name those providers make available; we never see your password on those providers.
  • Work log entries — timestamps, whether you were at the office or working from home, and any notes you add.
  • Settings you configure — required daily hours, days off, notification preferences, and, if you fill them in, optional company/employee/payroll details used only to generate your own reports.
  • Office location — only if you turn on GPS-based auto-tracking, we store the coordinates of the office location(s) you save, so the app can detect when you arrive/leave.
  • Push notification subscription — only if you enable browser push reminders, we store the subscription details your browser gives us so we can deliver those reminders.

How we use it

Solely to run the app: recording and displaying your work hours, generating your reports, and sending the reminders you've turned on. We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't run analytics or tracking scripts of any kind.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in a Supabase-hosted database. Each account's data is private to that account — other users of the app cannot see it.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or share your data for marketing. The only third parties involved are:

  • Supabase — hosts the database and handles authentication.
  • Google / GitHub — only if you choose to sign in with them, solely to verify your identity.
  • Your browser's push service (e.g. a Google or Mozilla push endpoint) — only if you enable notifications, solely to deliver them.

Data retention & deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, email taljacob2@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised accordingly.
